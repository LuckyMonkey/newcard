| File | What | Made with |
| --- | --- | --- |

The scenes are illustrations, not product screenshots: NewCard has no app yet. All cards shown are generic and unbranded on purpose. Do not add real transit-agency, bank or chip-vendor branding to these images.

Diagrams use a fixed light background so they stay readable in GitHub's light and dark themes. Fact references in the diagrams point to [`../FACTS.md`](../FACTS.md).
